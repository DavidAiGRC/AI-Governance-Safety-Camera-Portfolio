# AI Governance Portfolio: Industrial Safety Camera System

**Author: David Amoako-Attah**  
**Independent portfolio case study | Prepared August 8, 2026**

## Overview
This case study applies AI governance practices to an industrial safety camera scenario covering PPE detection, restricted zones, falls, and proximity hazards. It demonstrates risk assessment, ownership, human oversight, validation planning, worker transparency, incident response, and executive reporting.

## Start here
Begin with the executive presentation, then the NIST AI RMF gap assessment and risk register. The remaining artifacts show how proposed controls would be implemented and monitored.

## Key assessment finding
The scenario prioritizes site-specific accuracy and fairness validation across lighting, camera placement, PPE variation, and worker demographics. Supporting recommendations address accountable ownership, human review, worker appeals, controlled changes, and rollback authority.

## Skills demonstrated
- NIST AI RMF Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage assessment
- AI inventory and internal risk classification
- Risk register and remediation planning (POA&M)
- RACI and cross-functional accountability
- Model validation planning and performance monitoring
- AI incident response, rollback, and worker transparency
- Executive communication and governance dashboards

## Scope and limitations
This is an educational portfolio scenario, not an employer audit or evidence of a production deployment. Current-state descriptions, sample metrics/results, dates, and roles are illustrative or require validation. The validation workbook and dashboard must not be presented as independently verified model performance. Internal high-risk classification is a governance decision; jurisdiction-specific legal classification requires qualified review.

Some artifacts reference an Acceptable Use Policy, impact assessment, or vendor questionnaire that are not included in this ten-file package. Workbook references and dashboard figures should be reconciled before operational use. No employer or university endorsement is implied.

Download Office files to view their full formatting. Placeholders require replacement and proposed controls require stakeholder approval before real-world use.
